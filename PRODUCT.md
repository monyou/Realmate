# Realmate

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Couples and friend groups have equal priority. They come to Realmate when they want to choose a movie or series to watch together. One person hosts the room. The others can join as guests without creating accounts.

## Product Purpose

Help people settle on a title everyone wants to watch. A successful session ends when everyone likes the same title. If the deck runs out first, the group gets a no-match result.

## Operating Context

The host signs in and prepares private watch lists, either by adding titles manually or by generating a list from TMDB. They select one or more lists to form the room’s deck and share the room code. Voting can begin once at least two people are connected.

## Product Principles

- Make a two-person watch decision as straightforward as a group movie night.
- Let one person prepare the choices. Guests need only the room code to join.
- Give everyone the same deck and an equal vote. The first unanimous like determines the match.
